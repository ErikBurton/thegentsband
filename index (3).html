About

South Bay Rock, Done Right

  • HomeSan Jose, CA
  • PlaysSouth Bay & Bay Area
  • StyleClassic rock & covers
  • Lineup5 piece

The Gents have been a fixture of the San Jose and South Bay music scene for years. The band grew out of local clubs, backyard parties and street festivals, and still plays them with the same energy.

The set list covers the classic rock radio staples, the deep cuts and the sing-along anthems. Every show is built for a dance floor that fills up by the second song.

Want a particular song for your event? Ask when you book and we'll tell you if it's in the book.
